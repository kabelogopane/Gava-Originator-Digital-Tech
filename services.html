06 / PERSONAL DIGITAL PROFILES

Present your skills with confidence.

We help individuals turn their experience, education, skills and achievements into a professional digital presence. Whether you are looking for your first opportunity, changing careers, freelancing or building your own name, we help you present your story clearly and make it easier for people to understand what you can offer.

What we can create

  • Digital CVs: A clean, well-structured CV that presents your experience, education, skills and contact details professionally.
  • Online resumes: A digital version of your professional profile that is easy to share with employers and recruiters.
  • Personal portfolio websites: A dedicated page to introduce yourself, show your work and give visitors a way to contact you.
  • Graduate and job-seeker profiles: Help presenting studies, practical experience, projects, volunteering and transferable skills.
  • Skills and qualification profiles: A clear overview of your capabilities, training, certificates and areas of experience.
  • Project and achievement showcases: A place to demonstrate completed work, case studies, creative projects and milestones.
  • Personal branding layouts: Consistent colours, typography and presentation that help your profile look polished and recognisable.

Who this service is for

Students and graduates preparing to enter the job market, job seekers updating their CVs, professionals looking for their next move, freelancers showing examples of their work, and entrepreneurs who want a stronger personal profile.

What the process looks like

We first discuss your goal and the type of profile you need. You provide the correct information, experience and examples of your work. We organise the content, agree on a design, build the selected profile and give you an opportunity to review it before final delivery. The exact deliverables, revisions and any ongoing costs are agreed upfront.

Our aim: to help you present your real skills and experience clearly. A digital CV or portfolio can strengthen how you present yourself, but it cannot guarantee employment or opportunities.

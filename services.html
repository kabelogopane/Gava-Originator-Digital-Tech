Your website is often the first place a potential customer learns about you. We design clear, responsive websites that explain what you offer, show why it matters and guide visitors towards an enquiry or next step. For needs beyond a standard website, we can plan browser-based web applications around a specific task or business process.

What we can create

  • Business and company websites with clear service pages
  • Landing pages for products, services or campaigns
  • Online portfolios to showcase projects and work
  • Browser-based web applications and interactive tools
  • Online stores and product showcases, where scope allows
  • Contact forms, enquiry links and clear calls to action
  • Mobile-friendly layouts for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Website content updates and maintenance

Who it helps

Small businesses, start-ups, service providers, creatives and individuals who need a professional online presence or a simple digital tool.

What to expect

We discuss your audience and goals, agree on the pages and features, prepare the design, build and test the site, then explain the handover. Hosting, domain fees and third-party services are agreed separately where applicable.

  • Business and company websites
  • Landing pages and online portfolios
  • Web applications and interactive tools
  • Online stores and product showcases
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Website updates and maintenance

02 / CRM SYSTEMS

Keep important relationships organised.

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system gives a business one organised place to keep track of customers, enquiries, conversations and next steps. We help shape simple CRM setups around the way your team works, rather than adding features you do not need.

What we can set up

  • Customer, contact and company records
  • Lead and new-enquiry tracking
  • Candidate and job-application tracking
  • Programme, service or case tracking where relevant
  • Status fields and follow-up stages
  • Basic dashboards and activity reports
  • Salesforce setup, configuration and learning prototypes
  • Guidance for using and maintaining the system

Who it helps

Small teams that currently rely on scattered spreadsheets, notebooks or messages to remember customer details and track progress.

What to expect

We map the information you need to manage, agree on the records and stages, configure the agreed system and test it with sample data. Platform subscriptions, licences, migration and advanced integrations are scoped separately.

  • Customer and contact records
  • Lead and enquiry tracking
  • Candidate and application tracking
  • Follow-up and status management
  • Basic dashboards and reports
  • Salesforce configuration and prototypes

03 / AUTOMATION

Reduce repetitive steps.

Some tasks happen again and again: receiving an enquiry, recording details, reminding someone to follow up or moving information from one place to another. We look for practical ways to make these steps more consistent and reduce avoidable manual work.

What we can help improve

  • Enquiry and lead follow-up workflows
  • Task reminders and notifications
  • Form-to-spreadsheet processes
  • Moving information between agreed tools
  • Simple approval or handover steps
  • Repeated data-entry processes
  • Workflow reviews and improvement recommendations

Who it helps

Business owners and small teams who spend too much time repeating the same admin steps or chasing updates.

What to expect

We map the current process, identify where delays or repeated work happen, suggest a suitable improvement and test the agreed workflow. Automation depends on the tools, permissions and subscriptions available; not every process should be automated.

  • Enquiry and lead follow-up workflows
  • Task reminders and notifications
  • Form-to-spreadsheet processes
  • Data entry and handover improvements
  • Workflow reviews and recommendations

05 / DATA & REPORTING

Turn information into useful insight.

Business information is more useful when it is accurate, organised and easy to understand. We help structure spreadsheets and turn suitable data into clear summaries, reports and simple dashboards so you can see important activity at a glance.

What we can help with

  • Spreadsheet clean-up and consistent formatting
  • Organising lists, records and business information
  • Simple sales, customer or operations reports
  • Performance dashboards and summary views
  • Charts and visual summaries of key figures
  • Tracking agreed measures over time
  • Basic data-quality checks and tidy-up recommendations

Who it helps

Small businesses and teams that collect information but find it difficult to organise, summarise or use it to understand what is happening.

What to expect

We agree on the questions the report should answer, review the available data, organise it and prepare the agreed output. Reports are only as reliable as the information supplied, and sensitive personal data should be handled with appropriate permission and care.

  • Spreadsheet clean-up and organisation
  • Simple business reports
  • Performance dashboards
  • Data summaries and visual insights
  • Clearer tracking of key activity
